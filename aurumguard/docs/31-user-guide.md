# 31 User guide

1. Read the disclosure and register; complete onboarding (timezone, currency, paper equity, risk %, Friday cutoff, horizons).
2. Dashboard: check data health and the DEMO banner first. Each horizon shows one status. NO TRADE and WAIT are the most common and are correct outcomes.
3. A BUY/SELL SETUP card links to Setup details (all 39 fields) and the Evidence Inspector (what was used, what failed, what contradicts). Read the contradictory evidence and the confidence range with its sample size before doing anything.
4. Paper trading: "Take in paper account" places a simulated market order; the server re-checks every risk limit. Positions are managed automatically (stop, TP1 partial, TP2, time stop, Friday closure). Manual close is available.
5. Notifications: enable push in the Notification centre (iOS: install to Home Screen first). Quiet hours and throttles are in Settings. The centre is the record of everything sent or held.
6. Calendar: before a major USD event you get a scenario table with sample sizes; during the lockout no setups are issued; after verification the reaction is shown.
7. Risk: locks appear on the dashboard and stop new entries until they clear. Change limits in Settings within the shown bounds.
8. Research: the Strategy registry shows every rule; the Backtesting lab runs backtests/validations on the available data; Performance and Calibration show samples separately.
9. Friday: one hour before your cutoff you receive a review; weekly/swing paper positions close at the cutoff unless you recorded the weekend-risk override.
